Hermes Google Workspace is a private automation tool used by Unpack Leadership to support internal workflows involving Google Workspace.

The tool is used only with Google accounts that have been explicitly authorized by the account owner. Its purpose is to help with tasks such as accessing approved Google Drive files, reading permitted Workspace data, and supporting automated internal workflows.

Hermes Google Workspace is not offered as a public application or service. Access is limited to authorized users and accounts.

For questions about this application, contact: **unpackleadership.hermes@gmail.com**
